# Robottino's faces

The Live tab → face look offers two choices: Portrait and White robot. White robot shows only the head of the reference photo, with a black screen, light-blue eyes and a mouth with a tongue. It has neither the body nor the background of the photo. The pictures are 320×240, the same as the K151's declared screen.

The choice can be saved on the iPhone even offline. If the verified robot is connected it is sent; the "Applica al robot" button tries again. The confirmation follows the MOD's ACK and the style is kept in the robot's preferences. The factory software does not implement this protocol. If a photo is open on the display, go back to the face first. The choice on the iPhone does not prove it was applied to the robot.

Both faces have six poses: serene, happy, thoughtful, wink, mouth ajar and mouth open. Studio's previews follow the iPhone's choice. During calls the mouth is driven by the intensity of the PCM being played: it closes in silence and uses two openings as the volume changes. It is an amplitude animation, not phonetic synchronisation; the audio of the AI conversation on the robot is still to be integrated. The hearts of the stroke are still drawn over the active style.

For the white robot the head was generated taking the attached photo as a reference, then the six expressions were adapted to the head's screen. The earlier pictures are unchanged. Rendering, timing, memory needed and animation must be tried on the real K151. The MOD archive uses the documented community host and was not installed: before any flash, model, firmware and partitions must be verified and an explicit confirmation given.

The tests cover selection, validation, saving the preference, the six poses, going back to the previous style, audio and silence, the cloud check and the Swift client with a virtual robot. No trial simulates the visual perception or the acoustic quality of the physical device.

Touch: on the white robot, a touch on the head adds a short beep and a cascade of green digits for about 3 seconds, besides the existing decorations. The beep may be left out if the audio system is busy. The movements keep the protections of verification, calibration and local activation. The new Tocco tab simulates head and screen separately for both faces, with a preview sound and movement on the iPhone: it sends no command to the robot. The real behaviour and the rendering of the digits are to be tested on the physical device.


## Freckles (7 November 2026)

A third, realistic face, id `redhead`: a girl with red hair and freckles, generated by the user with ChatGPT as a 3×2 grid of the six expressions (`firmware/stackchan-mod/assets/red-atlas-source.png`). `firmware/stackchan-mod/prepare-redhead.swift` cuts the six 320×240 poses (`red-*.png`), with the head aligned on the first one within 1 px. In the app the pictures are `Face-Red-*`. The same id is accepted by the app, the relay (`src/robot-wire.ts`), the MOD (`FACE_STYLES` in `portrait-state.js`) and the Live protocol. The MOD package goes from 2.5 to 3.7 MB: check that it fits the K151's partition before flashing.

Smooth mouth (7 November 2026): in the "mouth ajar" and "mouth open" poses of Freckles, ChatGPT had also changed eyes and eyebrows, so the whole face jumped at every syllable. `prepare-redhead.swift` now keeps the serene face and replaces only an oval (from under the nose to the chin, beyond the corners of the mouth) blended at the edges. In the app the conversation uses a continuous opening (`mouthOpenness`, 0–1) and cross-fades the mouth poses instead of snapping; the robot stays at three steps (`mouthLevel`), but without the jump of the face.

An even smoother mouth, and the blonde (7 November 2026): in the app the mouth opening goes through `RobotMouthSmoother` (it opens quickly, closes more gently, computed at every frame) and the mouth poses overlap with wider cross-fades. The Portrait (the blonde) now also keeps the serene face while talking: `firmware/stackchan-mod/prepare-blonde-mouth.swift` takes only the oval of the mouth from `girl-talk-*-full.png` (the original poses, kept) and writes `girl-talk-*.png` and the `Face-talk-*` image sets.
